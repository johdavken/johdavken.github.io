How Smart Hoppers work

Smart Hoppers automatically uses cylindrical dimensions (shared hopper circumference and each hopper’s usable height) or known usable volume, whichever method the connected line uses, together with the assigned recipe resin’s measured bulk density. Manual weights remain available at all times. Smart Hoppers requires a connected workspace with an identified line.

    Changelog
    What’s new, most recent first — the Privacy Policy and how we handle your data are also linked here

    (v1.1.20) (August 28, 2026) - Timeline Hookup planning

    • The Timeline gains a Hookups tab: record the silo/box-line source for each resin in the Current and Next recipe, and those short labels appear inline on Timeline rows (A0700 [A] → A0711 [C]). Assignments are grouped by resin, saved automatically, and shared over RT Sync.
    • A gear beside Show all opens Timeline Display - one place to show or hide the Next resin and to choose whether source labels appear for Current or Current + Next. The old standalone Enhanced tracking switch is folded in as Next resin.

    (v1.1.19) (August 27, 2026) - Recipe toolbar redesign, theme-aware pill colors & admin conflict-storm visibility

    • Recipe's header pill and Edit toolbar merge into single right-anchored, segmented-pill rows on tablet/desktop, fixing several width/wrap bugs only visible on real touch tablets and narrow desktop windows.
    • Recipe pill buttons (Edit/Done, Load Next/Print, Clear/Empty/Reset/Rearrange) switch to a theme-aware tinted fill instead of a flat accent, so they no longer overpower dark or bright themes.
    • Mobile Recipe's Edit toolbar is lighter and no longer overlaps on short screens; the redundant per-layer Total 100% readout is dropped on mobile (kept on tablet/desktop).
    • Fixed Clear tracking's border staying visible on tablet and narrow desktop windows, and Industrial Slate's mobile home screen text reading too dark against its own pale theme.
    • Admin Database Health now surfaces RT Sync conflict storms directly, with a badge, a list, and a one-click jump to the affected workspace in Workspace Management.

    (v1.1.17) (August 25-26, 2026) - Compact mobile/desktop Recipe & Weights, workspace nav polish

    • Status bar Output/Changeover are now click-to-edit directly, instead of buried in the Recipe nav button; Changelog is promoted to its own top-level panel and Help is removed, its essential links folded into Workspace & support.
    • Mobile Recipe/Weights cleanup: removed Select all everywhere, unified Rearrange into the Edit toolbar on every width, and shrunk Weights' mobile cells to match Recipe's density.
    • Desktop Recipe Summary no longer reserves dead space for Edit's row-selector column, and mobile's Clear tracking moved into the main action row instead of its own bar.
    • Wide-desktop Recipe layer headers are far more compact: a large percentage reading, a small layer-letter tag, and a colour-coded total bar in place of the old 64px watermark letter.
    • Fixed Weights' Edit toggle silently going stale after touching Smart Hoppers, and an Android update issue that could leave stale web assets in place after installing a new version.

    (v1.1.16) (August 24-25, 2026) - Recipe workflow, workspace navigation & RT Sync hardening

    • Recipe now keeps a stable grid, header, and footer across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with in-place mobile/tablet editing, Undo/Redo, and percentages that no longer clip.
    • Added Recipe Book alongside Current and Next, with Load Current Recipe and Save Next Recipe available from either page.
    • Redesigned Resin Totals on desktop and tablet with fixed material/lot/pounds columns and a clear Total issued row.
    • Simplified mobile navigation to Display, Main, and Alerts, with Sudo access moved into Workspace & support; added System / Auto and OLED Black themes and restored Nord.
    • Hardened RT Sync against stale-client revision storms that could keep retrying against out-of-date state.

    (v1.1.14) (August 23, 2026) - Mobile workspace, Recipe editing & display

    • Redesigned the mobile Home screen as a compact connected workflow rail, with a slim text-only footer command bar.
    • Made Recipe tracking and editing visually distinct: a notched hopper badge for tracked cells, a small Edit tag for selection.
    • Compressed the tablet Recipe header and Edit controls into two stable rows.
    • Restored Rosé Pine Light, Evergreen, and Evergreen Light display themes.

    (v1.1.10) (August 22, 2026) - Mobile Home navigation

    • Reorganized the mobile Home screen as a connected, numbered production rail. RT Sync, Tools, and Help now sit behind one compact Workspace & support disclosure.

    (v1.1.9) (August 21-22, 2026) - Changeover planning & Recipe cell actions

    • Recipe Setup can now copy in both directions: Load Current Recipe on the Next page seeds a changeover plan from what is already running.
    • Added Empty to Edit view, clearing a hopper's resin, percentage, and Track setting in one action — previously impossible on mobile.
    • Fixed hopper cells only being selectable from their top half, and added bulk Clear tracking / Clear selection controls.
    • Added Enhanced tracking to the Timeline, showing each tracked hopper's incoming resin change ahead of a changeover.
    • Fixed the Android pump-off alarm being impossible to silence and not surviving a phone restart; Android downloads now go through a beta access request.

    (v1.1.6) (August 19-20, 2026) - Summary/Edit views & RT Sync reliability

    • Recipe Setup and Receiver Hopper Weights now share one Summary / Edit mode on every device, with touch editing routed through the panel instead of direct cell typing.
    • Fixed RT Sync conflict storms where two devices pushed competing job versions back and forth and could leave a panel stuck disabled.
    • Fixed the Smart Hoppers panel not refreshing when a line connects.

    (v1.1.1) (August 16-17, 2026) - Database health, hopper geometry & tools

    • Added the admin-only Database Health monitor for the Supabase backend.
    • Added the Hopper Volume Weight tool and a matching Smart Hoppers volume geometry mode for lines whose hoppers are not simple cylinders.
    • Refined Bulk Density Measurement and Gruvbox theme styling; fixed the Android launcher icon and mobile notification actions.

    • Published a public Privacy Policy and Data Deletion page, both linked from the bottom of this guide.

    • Desktop heads-up banners were replaced by a single notification center, and the default theme was renamed Industrial Slate.

    Smart Hopper, RT Sync & admin refinements - August 11, 2026

    • Line 9 now automatically uses Main + 1–5 hopper naming while linked, including when temporarily offline. Other lines, local-only workspaces, and disconnected devices use 1–6; changing the label convention never changes saved hopper assignments.
    • Added the admin-only Bulk Density Measurement tool. It calculates measured bulk density and packing factor from the calibrated container, can update only the selected resin's bulk-density value after confirmation, and immediately refreshes Resin Reference and Smart Hopper calculations.
    • Refined Receiver Hopper Weights and Recipe Setup with denser, more readable mobile matrices, clearer Smart Hopper states, and consistent action bars for profiles, recipes, bulk editing, and rearranging.

    • Mobile now opens to a focused tile home instead of a long card list. Each section opens on its own, with a consistent return-to-tiles control; Tools and Help also have their own tile indexes.
    • Added a mobile Appearance tile with five tile treatments and two background options. Minimal is now the default tile treatment, and both Gruvbox Light and Gruvbox Dark receive matching warm, textured backgrounds and retro-styled tiles.
    • Added a timeline option to alert when a tracked hopper reaches Pump off, with sound, vibration, an in-app alert, and notifications where the browser permits them.
    • Added the animated Quick Actions button on the mobile tile home, providing one-tap access to Scan Dosing Screen and Production Summary.
    • Improved mobile navigation and polish throughout: section and tool headings are clearer, Scan Recipe controls are easier to use, and every main tile - including Admin Login - has a matching SVG icon.

    Recipe Setup polish, Timeline cleanup & readability - August 7, 2026

    • Changed the default theme to Industrial Slate. Also tried out several Header style options (Bold Slab, Editorial Serif, Rounded Grotesk, Wide Display) via a temporary picker, then removed the picker and kept Wide Display as the permanent header typography.

    Recipe Scanner & mobile polish - August 5-6, 2026

    • Added Scan Recipe: reads a photo of a Job Traveler, a Dosing Screen, or a Heat Sheet and fills Recipe Setup automatically, with a review screen before anything is applied. A camera-icon shortcut in the mobile status bar jumps straight to it.
    • RT Sync now shows exactly which changes haven't synced yet, with a way to discard one that's stuck against an unreachable line. Tapping the mobile footer status refreshes and retries.
    • Recipe Setup on mobile: cards open one at a time instead of stacking up, collapsed card titles are bigger with the subtitle hidden until opened, swipe left/right to switch layers, hopper rows now fit on a single line, and Layers A/B can copy from E/D (in addition to C/D/E copying from A/B).

    • Added a desktop Print Recipe button.
    • Added a warning when the changeover time looks stale instead of silently rolling it forward to the next day.
    • Five more color themes (Rosé Pine, Rosé Pine Dawn, Everforest, Everforest Light, One Dark) and a Header style preference.
    • Reworked mobile's Bulk Edit and Rearrange Hoppers to support tap-to-move, not just drag.
    • Ongoing RT Sync reliability work to cut unnecessary syncing and keep multiple devices in sync smoothly.
