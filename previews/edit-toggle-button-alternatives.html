Edit toggle button: alternatives

On Receiver Hopper Weights (and Recipe's own header at narrower widths) the Edit/Done toggle has no sibling action next to it - it's the only thing in the header's right side, and today it's a plain outlined rectangle: transparent fill, a barely-there 20%-opacity border, small dark text. Every mockup below reproduces that exact context - the Weights panel's title, tab strip, and the button alone on the right - so each candidate is judged in the actual spot it will sit.

All ten reuse var(--focus-border), the same per-theme accent Scan/Print already draw from on mobile - so whichever direction you pick will automatically follow every theme, not just Industrial Slate shown here.

Animated alternatives

6-9 loop automatically so they're visible without touching anything; 7 and 9 are hover/focus-triggered - point at them (or tab to them with the keyboard). All four looping ones respect prefers-reduced-motion and freeze in their resting state for anyone with that setting on.

A light streak sweeps across the filled pill every few seconds, the way a "premium" or "new feature" button sometimes shines. Highest-personality option here - probably reads as too promotional for a plain mode toggle an operator uses many times a shift.

My lean: #1 (accent-filled pill) if you want it static, or #7 (hover fill sweep) if you want motion - they're the same pill at rest, #7 just adds the fill as a hover payoff instead of a permanent state. Both give the button real visual weight without asking for continuous attention. #2 (icon-only circle) is worth a look too since it directly extends the icon language Scan/Print already established for mobile actions, though it trades the explicit "Edit" label for a glyph people have to learn once. Of the two idle-looping options that keep moving forever (#6's glow, #10's shimmer), I'd skip both for a control seen dozens of times a shift - constant motion on something that routine gets tuned out or gets annoying, not noticed. #8's icon wiggle is the gentler middle ground if you want some idle personality without going that far.
