NOTES

What each one is

01

Radar

One thin ring leaves the dot, grows, and fades. The stroke stays hairline as it grows (the ring's box is animated, not its transform). One breath, a pause, again.

02

Double ring

Two rings half a cycle apart, so one is always on its way out. Busier; reads at a glance from across the room.

03

Sonar

The radar ring, and the dot itself brightens the instant the ring leaves - the "contact" flash before the sweep.

04

Halo

No ring travels. The dot's own halo swells and settles, the way the RESET word breathes when it is armed. The quietest of the four.

—

Intensity

Whisper 19 px reach · 3 s · 35 %. Standard 26 px · 2.2 s · 60 %. Insistent 34 px · 1.5 s · 85 %. Lanes are 16 px apart, so Standard's ring just crosses the neighbour's dot before it is gone.

—

Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (or Motion off, above) nothing travels: a late dot wears one still ring at rest.
