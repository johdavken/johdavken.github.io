Privacy Policy

Last updated August 14, 2026

Resin.Tools is a production-floor tool for blown film operators. It plans hopper run-downs, holds recipe and line setup, and can share that job state between the devices working one line. This policy covers the Resin.Tools website at resin.tools and the Resin Tools Android app.

Using Resin.Tools does not require an account. Everything below that leaves your device happens only in the specific features described.

What stays on your device

Most of what you enter is kept in your browser's local storage, on the device you entered it on:

  • Line rate, changeover time, and layer/hopper setup
  • Recipes — resin names and codes, hopper blend percentages, layer percentages, and resin lot numbers
  • Receiver hopper weights
  • Production and scrap values
  • Tracking and pump-off selections, and timeline state
  • Display preferences — theme, density, time format
  • Configurations saved locally on this device
  • A cached copy of the resin catalog, so the app works with a poor connection

This data is held by your browser and is not sent anywhere unless you use RT Sync or Scan Recipe. Clearing site data for resin.tools in your browser — or clearing storage for Resin Tools in Android settings — removes it from that device.

RT Sync and shared workspaces

RT Sync is off until you create or join a workspace. When you connect, the app signs your browser in to an anonymous account with Supabase, the hosted backend Resin.Tools runs on. Anonymous means what it says: no email address, no name, no password, and nothing to sign up for. The identity exists only so the server can tell devices apart, and it is stored only in that browser.

While a workspace is connected, this is stored on Supabase servers:

  • The workspace name and the line it represents
  • A device ID generated by the app, and a device label — it defaults to "Operator Phone" or "Resin.Tools Desktop" and you can rename it
  • Membership records: which anonymous identity belongs to which workspace, when it joined, and when it was last active
  • The shared active job — the same recipe, setup, tracking and timeline values listed in the previous section
  • Shared configurations you explicitly save: recipes and receiver weight profiles, and the names you give them

Every member of a workspace can read and change that workspace's data. Treat device labels, workspace names, configuration names, and recipe fields as visible to everyone else on that line.

Disconnecting stops this device from syncing, and leaving a workspace removes this device's membership. Neither deletes the workspace itself — its data stays with the members who remain.

Scan Recipe

Scan Recipe — Scan Job Traveler, Scan Dosing Screen, and Scan Heat Sheet — runs only when you choose it, and only while you are connected to a workspace. When you take or select a photo:

  1. The image is sent to a Resin.Tools server function, hosted on Supabase, together with your workspace ID. The function verifies that your anonymous identity is a member of that workspace before doing anything else.
  2. The function sends the image to OpenAI's API, which reads it and returns the values it can make out: layer positions and percentages, resin codes, hopper blend percentages, hopper designations, and lot numbers where the form carries them.
  3. Those values come back to your device as a draft for you to review. Nothing is written to your recipe until you confirm it.

The scan function does not save the image or the extracted result to the Resin.Tools database. For up to a minute after a scan, the result is held in that function's memory so that retrying the same request does not reprocess the photo. The function's own server logs record extracted values, such as resin codes and layer percentages, for troubleshooting; they do not record the image.

Photos are processed by Supabase and OpenAI as part of providing this feature, under their own policies: Supabase, OpenAI.

Whatever is in the frame is part of the photo and is sent for processing, so frame the document you are scanning.

Camera and photos

On Android, Resin.Tools asks for camera access when you choose to take a photo for a scan, and for access to your photos when you choose an existing one. Both are requested at the moment you use them. The app does not take photos in the background and does not browse your photo library on its own.

Notifications

Timeline alerts are scheduled on the device itself. They are not push notifications — no notification service is involved, and no data leaves your device to deliver one.

Administrator sign-in

Editing the shared resin database is restricted to administrators, who sign in with an email address and password held by Supabase Auth. Administrator permission is checked on the server for every such action, not merely hidden in the interface. Ordinary use of Resin.Tools never involves signing in.

Advertising and sale of data

Resin.Tools does not serve ads. It does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. There is no analytics, tracking, or advertising software in the app or on the website.

Children

Resin.Tools is a workplace tool for production staff. It is not directed to children.

Removing data

  • On your device — clear site data for resin.tools in your browser, or clear storage for Resin Tools in Android settings.
  • A shared recipe or receiver weight profile — delete it from within the app. It is removed from the workspace for every member.
  • A workspace — Leave RT Sync removes this device's membership. To have a workspace or its stored job data removed, get in touch using the contact below.

Changes to this policy
