Privacy Policy

Request Data Deletion

Most Resin.Tools data can be removed by you, from the device or from inside the app. Workspace data shared across a line has to be removed by us, because deleting a whole workspace is an administrator action.

Deletion requests

johdavken@protonmail.com

What you can remove yourself

Data on your device. Clear site data for resin.tools in your browser, or clear storage for Resin Tools in Android settings. That removes the locally held job and setup data — line rate, recipes, receiver hopper weights, production values, tracking state, display preferences, locally saved configurations, and the cached resin catalog — from that device.

Shared recipes and receiver weight profiles. Select the saved recipe or receiver weight profile in Resin.Tools, open its ⋯ menu, and choose Delete. It is removed from the shared workspace for every member, not just from your device.

RT Sync membership. The Leave RT Sync control removes this device's membership of the workspace. Your local Resin.Tools data is preserved, and the workspace itself is not deleted — it stays with the members who remain.

What to ask us to remove

A workspace and its shared job data. Email the address above. Deleting a workspace removes the data owned by that workspace, including its shared active job, saved setups, shared recipes and receiver weight profiles, and the membership records for every device that joined it.

So the right workspace can be identified, include what you know of:

  • The workspace name
  • The line it represents
  • The device label shown for your device, if you know it

That is all that is needed. Please do not send personal details, account credentials, or production documents — none of them help identify a workspace.

Scan Recipe images

There is nothing to delete. A photo submitted through Scan Recipe is processed for that scan and returned to your device as a draft; the scan function does not save the image or the extracted result to the Resin.Tools database. This is described in full under Scan Recipe in the Privacy Policy.

What clearing your device does not remove

Clearing browser or app storage discards that device's RT Sync session credential. It does not remove the anonymous authentication record held in Supabase, and it does not remove anything already stored in a shared workspace — that data belongs to the workspace, not to your device. Use the sections above for either of those.
